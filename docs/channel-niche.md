# Channel niche rules (creator, Nov 24, 2026)

## CashleyFinance
Strictly money, investing and the economy, including sub-niches:
- Investing and markets (stocks, index funds, crashes, trading psychology, IPOs, earnings).
- Personal finance (saving, debt, credit cards, credit scores, car loans, budgeting, taxes, inflation).
- The economy and money history (banks, inflation, the Fed, money supply, how money/rules were invented).
- Business models and the money side of everyday things (how companies make money, pricing, scams, crypto as money).

Not on CashleyFinance:
- Sports and basketball (they go on the separate basketball channel, scripts in scripts/hoops/).
- Gambling and sports betting content (e.g. the parlay video, scripts/shorts/24-parlay/, is off-niche for CashleyFinance).
- Anything that isn't primarily about money, investing or the economy.

Check every new idea against this list before pitching it.

## Basketball channel (separate)
Animated, original basketball stories (history, rules, myths, players), no real game footage or logos. Lives in scripts/hoops/.
