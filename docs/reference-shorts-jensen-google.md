# Reference shorts: "Jensen Huang's Affair Rocks NVIDIA" and "How Google Will Win the AI Race"

Analysed November 13, 2026 from the creator's downloads (Clipto logo in the last 2 to 3 s ignored). Same channel style: 3D stick figures, second-person scripts, burned-in 1 to 2 word captions, 608x1080.

## Jensen (about 150 s, a cut every 0.86 s, 176 cuts)
Eight acts, each closed by a punchline that opens the next:
1. Throne (0:00): "You are Jensen Huang... king of the AI Gold Rush... everyone digs for gold, but you are selling the shovels... $5 trillion."
2. Flex: employees drive Lambos, garage like a car showroom.
3. Flashback: teenage dishwasher at Denny's, starts NVIDIA in a Denny's, nearly bankrupt, survives.
4. The bet nobody believed: CUDA 2006, "everyone says it's stupid, for years."
5. Vindication: 2012 image contest, the world needs chips, everyone pays you. "Not bad for a dishwasher."
6. Twist: AMD's Lisa Su is his cousin; "the AI race is basically a family business."
7. The fall: "But every king has a weakness." Export ban, alleged smuggling, NYT spy probe, affair, second relationship, FBI, $200 billion in a day, resigns after 33 years.
8. Callback: "the man who sold shovels to the world... turns out he was digging his own grave."

Mechanics: viewer is the protagonist ("You are..."); one metaphor (shovels) set up early and flipped at the end; punchline at every act change; full-zero shock numbers ($5,000,000,000,000; $200,000,000,000); crown as a status symbol that disappears; constant camera motion; one real photo for credibility.

## Google (about 114 s, a cut every 2.4 s)
