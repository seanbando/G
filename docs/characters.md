# Channel characters

## YOU: the recurring main character (creator, 2026-11-13)

The viewer's stand-in in every short; scripts talk to "you" and he acts it out.

- **Look:** young guy about 22, short messy brown hair, orange hoodie, jeans, white sneakers, friendly simple cartoon face, often holding a phone.
- **StrokeVideo:** saved as a character (no reference photo), drawn in the same style as the videos. Any video that uses him costs about 100 StrokeVideo credits a minute instead of 50.
- **Golpo and other tools:** paste the look above word for word into the visual instructions; check every board for drift, because Golpo does not lock characters.
- **Roles he plays:** the viewer's question line (YOU voice, Eric), reactions (shocked, eyes popping), holding money or a phone, older self when a script looks ahead.
- **Never:** a real person's face, a logo on his hoodie.

## Supporting cast (described per video, not saved)
- **The boss:** simple man in a tie.
- **News anchor:** TV anchor at a desk with a red BREAKING banner (Andrew's voice).
