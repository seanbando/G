# Channel characters

## YOU: the recurring main character (creator, 2026-11-13)

The viewer's stand-in in every short; scripts talk to "you" and he acts it out.

- **Look (creator's reference sheet, 2026-11-13):** young man, messy spiky black hair, black square glasses, small stud earrings, warm skin tone, friendly smile; black suit, white shirt, thin black tie, white pocket square, black dress shoes. Clean cartoon marker style. (Replaces the earlier orange-hoodie description.)
- **StrokeVideo:** upload `assets/characters/you-reference.png` as the reference picture when creating the character (the reference-picture option costs 120 credits instead of 40), drawn in the same style as the videos. Any video that uses him costs about 100 StrokeVideo credits a minute instead of 50.
- **Reference sheet:** `assets/characters/you-reference.png`, the creator's sheet with a front portrait, side profile and full body. The old phone-guy sheet is kept as `you-reference-old-phone-guy.png` and is no longer used. Pass it to Golpo in `upload_urls` and say "YOU = the man in the uploaded reference image" in the visual instructions.
- **Golpo and other tools:** paste the look above word for word into the visual instructions; check every board for drift, because Golpo does not lock characters.
- **Roles he plays:** the viewer's question line (YOU voice, Eric), reactions (shocked, eyes popping), holding money or a phone, older self when a script looks ahead.
- **Never:** a real person's face, logos on his clothes, a different hairstyle or no glasses.
