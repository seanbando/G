# Hook bank (adapted from topicfinder.ai's 100 hooks, Nov 2026)

Pick ONE hook, inside the ONE structure the idea uses. Every hook must stay nerd-proof and be paid off in the video.

## Best fits for us
| Type | Our version (examples) | Fits structure |
|---|---|---|
| Mind reading | "Let me guess. You paid the minimum, then the next minimum, then wondered why the balance barely moved." / "You know you should invest. You just haven't." | Money Mystery |
| Labeling | "If you've ever paid only the minimum, this is for you." / "If you're 22 with your first $1,000, watch this twice." | Money Mystery |
| Stakes | "The minimum payment is cheap today. It's brutal in year five." / "Most people find out about this when the statement comes." | Money Mystery |
| Curiosity gap | "There's a box on your credit card statement that's costing you money if you ignore it." / "A cable and a microwave tower decided who won on Wall Street." | Ricks / Money Mystery |
| Contrarian | "Stop checking your investments every day. It's why people sell at the bottom." / "Broke people aren't bad with money." | Money Mystery |
| Proof hijack | "Studies say almost 4 in 10 adults couldn't cover a $400 surprise." / "The data on market crashes is clear. Most advice ignores it." | Any (real sources only) |
| Promise stack / named mechanism | "There's a name for why selling in a crash costs you. I call it the shoebox mistake." | Money Mystery |
| Common enemy (fair) | "Your card company makes more when you pay the minimum." (true: more interest) / "Fees you never see are eating your returns." | Money Mystery |

## Use carefully or skip
- Story lead-in ("I was 22 when I learned...") needs a real first-person story; we narrate as a channel. Use only with a true story, or as YOU's story clearly framed as an example.
- "I've read 50 books / studied 100 hours" proof hooks: only if literally true.
- Gurus/industry enemy hooks: never accuse a named person or company without facts.
- Framework hooks ("The 3-bucket method") work for list videos, not Ricks stories.
