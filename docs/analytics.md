**Reading:** the hook works (low skip rate). Viewers leave around 0:20, just after the anchor bulletin and before the "paper wealth" payoff at 0:27. Weak likes and shares stopped the push; nothing invited comments or follows.

**Changes for next shorts:**
1. First answer by about 0:15; keep the twist for later.
2. Keep news set-ups to one quick line.
3. End on a question viewers want to answer, and repeat it in the caption.
4. Caption line that invites sharing ("Send this to someone who just got their first job").
5. A series name and the recurring YOU character to give a reason to follow.
