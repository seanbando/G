# Idea Scorecard

Score each 1–5, multiply by the weight, add up. Max 100.

| Criteria | Weight | 5 = | 1 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| Competition (low is good) | ×5 | No big creator has a good long-form take | fern / That Chapter / Netflix covered it recently |
| Demand | ×4 | Existing videos on the case pull millions | Nobody searches it |
| Outlier proof | ×4 | A small channel already broke out with it | Only big channels get views on it |
| Originality | ×3 | We have a "hidden thing" angle nobody leads with | Same angle as everyone |
| Footage | ×2 | Government footage, court exhibits, press conferences, photos | Barely any visuals |
| Depth | ×2 | Easily fills 18–22 minutes | A 6-minute story |

Competition is weighted highest because a small channel can't outrank 60 Minutes or Netflix on a case they own.
