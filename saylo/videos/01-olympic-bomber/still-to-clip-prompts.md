# 01 — Turn the AI stills into clips (image-to-video)

Use the same tool as scenes A–J (Kling / Runway / Veo): **upload the still as the first frame**, paste the motion prompt, **5 s, 16:9, 1080p**.
Save each result with the same name plus `clip`, e.g. `loc-murphy-street clip.mp4`, and upload to the clips folder.

Paste this at the end of every prompt:
`subtle realistic motion, slow cinematic camera move, keep the scene and lighting exactly as the image, no new people, no faces, no text`

Real news photos (the AJC archive, Commons, press-conference stills) stay as photos: never animate a real photo of a real person into fake motion. They get the template effects instead (film window, case board, press stack, profiles).
