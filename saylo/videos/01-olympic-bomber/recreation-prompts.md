# 01 — Recreation clip prompts (K–AD)

The competitors use moving recreations for about half their screen time. Scenes A–J cover part of that; these 20 fill the rest.
Make them exactly like A–J: same tool (Kling / Runway / Veo), same look, **5 s clips, 16:9, 1080p**, save as `K clip.mp4`, `L clip.mp4`, and so on.
Upload them to the clips folder; Claude places them on the narration.

**Rules for every clip (same as A–J):**
- Nobody's face is visible: from behind, silhouettes, hands, shadows, or out of focus. Never a recognizable real person.
- No blood, no injured people, no explosions with victims.
- No readable text, logos or license plate numbers (Claude adds text in the edit).
- Style suffix to paste at the end of every prompt:
  `cinematic, photoreal, 1990s, dark moody grade, teal shadows, warm amber practical lights, film grain, shallow depth of field, slow camera move, no faces visible, no text`
