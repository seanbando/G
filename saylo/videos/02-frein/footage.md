## New bits found while digging — optional add-ins for the script
These are real and sourced; none are in the script yet. Each adds 10–25 seconds. Run any you want through GPT before recording.

**A. The funeral and the Most Wanted list (The Search, after "…took part.")**
> The FBI added Frein to its Ten Most Wanted list in late September, hours after thousands of officers and mourners attended Bryon Dickson's funeral. He was the five hundred and third person ever put on that list, with a reward of up to a hundred thousand dollars.
Sources: FBI Philadelphia release; NBC "FBI adds trooper slay suspect" (n206661); Police Magazine. Footage: funeral honor guard + FBI poster. Verify exact date.

**B. What the FBI's own poster said (Who They Were Looking For, after the reenactor line)**
> The FBI's wanted poster added details you don't usually see on one. It said he'd studied Russian and Serbian, and that he was known to be a heavy smoker.
Source: FBI Ten Most Wanted poster (public). (The poster also says he "claims to have fought with the Serbians in Africa" — strange enough that I'd only use it word-for-word, attributed to the poster, or skip it.)

**C. The Halloween payoff with a real voice (The Hangar, replaces the current Halloween payoff)**
> Earlier in the month, Barrett Township had moved Halloween into a school parking lot, a "trunk-or-treat." The night Frein was caught, township supervisor Ralph Megliola got the word out: trick-or-treating was on, the next night, from six to eight.
Sources: AP via NBC/CBS/Fox ("trick-or-treating is on tomorrow night from 6 to 8").

**D. Jeep details where accounts differ (no change needed)**
Early AP reports listed a 2001 Jeep Cherokee with his Social Security card, a Game Commission range permit, face paint and two empty rifle cases. The script follows the court opinion (black rifle case, two spent casings, license). Keep it that way.
