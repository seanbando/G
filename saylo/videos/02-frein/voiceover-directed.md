## Beat notes (★ = music out)
- "I saw the black hat." → quote it plainly, like reading his words, 1s pause after.
- "Eric Frein." → alone, flat. 1.2s pause. Let it land once.
- "And the troopers put something on him that belonged to Bryon Dickson." → slow, slight drop. "We'll get there." → conversational, lighter.
- "Hold onto that. It comes back at the end of this story." (the cruiser) → direct to viewer.
- "A rifle shot comes out of the dark…" → no emphasis, steady. 0.8s pause after.
- "Officers and paramedics worked on Bryon Dickson, and they couldn't save him." → slowest line of Part 1. 1s pause.
- "Got a shot around eleven p.m. and took it." → read as a quote, colder, no performance.
- "What they didn't have was Eric Frein." → ★ end of section, 1.2s pause.
- "Hold onto that recorded statement…" → lean in, lower.
- Halloween line ("trick-or-treating was canceled") → softer; "Remember that. It comes back too." → small smile.
- Part 2 teaser ("there was a notebook at that hangar") → near-aside.
- "Then the search went on for three more weeks." → flat, tired.
- Journal quotes ("slept like a human…", "Thirty days. A lot longer than I expected to last.") → ★ quietest reading in the video, 1s pause after each.
- "Those dates don't tell us when any particular line was written…" → careful, honest, a touch faster.
- Halloween payoff ("trick-or-treating could go ahead after all") → warm, a small release.
- "Before we go back to that night, here's how this ended in court." → reset, neutral.
- "They kept going." → clipped. 0.8s pause.
- "The rifle from the hangar… Frein's DNA was on it. The handwriting… was his." → three steady beats, equal weight.
- "Now go back to October thirtieth…" → slower, lower; the video starts to close.
- "And the troopers who took him put Corporal Bryon Dickson's handcuffs on him." → ★ 1.5s pause after.
- Final line: "Forty-eight days after Corporal Bryon Dickson was killed outside Blooming Grove, his patrol car pulled back into the barracks. Eric Frein was in the back seat, wearing Dickson's handcuffs." → ★ slowest pace of the video, no music, then **4–6 seconds of silence on the cruiser shot** before the outro.
- Outro → separate segment, normal warm energy, music back in quietly.
