## Rules while downloading
- Skip anything with music playing under it, and skip Daily Mail and compilation re-uploads.
- No footage of Dickson's body, the scene that night, or close-ups of the bombs.
- Don't blur or crop out the station's logo to hide it. Frame naturally; our watermark credits them.
- Put everything in `saylo/videos/02-frein/footage/` and tick the box. Note the clip's in/out time in `footage.md` → "Pick".
