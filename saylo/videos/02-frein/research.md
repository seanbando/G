# 02 — Eric Frein: Research (Nov 18, 2026)

Built from web search summaries of contemporaneous reporting (AP via multiple outlets, CBS, NBC, WNEP, WHYY, TNOnline, CS Monitor) and trial coverage (2017). Direct page fetches to foxnews.com were blocked from this session; the AP capture account was confirmed through other outlets carrying it.

## 1. The title fact — CONFIRMED ✅
- After the U.S. Marshals turned Frein over to state police, he was **placed in Cpl. Bryon Dickson's handcuffs and driven in Dickson's patrol car to the Blooming Grove barracks** — where Dickson was killed. Stated by State Police Commissioner **Frank Noonan**; Gov. **Tom Corbett** called it "very appropriate." (WHYY; TNOnline; Fox 13 "arrested in slain officer's cuffs"; multiple AP carriers)
- Then taken to Pike County Correctional Facility after processing. (Star-Advertiser/AP)

## 2. The ambush — Friday, September 12, 2014 ✅
- Pennsylvania State Police barracks, **Blooming Grove, Pike County**. Late-night **shift change**, about **10:50–11 p.m.**
- **Cpl. Bryon K. Dickson II, 38**, of Dunmore, shot walking toward his patrol car; died at the scene. Married, father of two young sons. (Marine veteran — NBC/AP sentencing coverage ✅)
- **Trooper Alex Douglass, 31**, of Olyphant, arriving for his shift, saw Dickson on the ground near the lobby, was shot (pelvis), critically wounded. He **crawled into the lobby**; another trooper pulled him to a secure area. ⚠️ (one source summary; repeated in "5 years later" WNEP coverage) — Hospitalized until Oct 16; by spring 2015 facing his 16th surgery. He testified at trial (UPI, Apr 2017).
- Weapon: **.308-caliber rifle**.
- Frein's journal: he "got a shot around 11 p.m. and took it." / "He dropped… I was surprised at how quick." (read at trial by ADA Bruce DeSarro; Reuters/WNEP) ✅
- Planning: in his confession he said he used **Google Earth** to scope out barracks near his home and picked Blooming Grove because it was **surrounded by woods and offered cover**. (AP "things we learned from confession") ✅ Police said he'd planned **for years** (CS Monitor, Sept 27 2014) ✅

## 3. How they identified him ✅
- About **three days later**, a man **walking his dog** found a **Jeep partly submerged in a swampy retention pond** about **2 miles** from the barracks and called 911. (ABC; WNEP "man who found Frein's Jeep")
- Inside: **shell casings matching the barracks scene**, **Frein's driver's license**, **camouflage face paint**, **two empty rifle cases**, military gear. (ABC, AP)
- Journal: he drove the Jeep into a swamp in a botched getaway and called it a "**disaster**," then continued on foot. ✅

## 4. Who he was
- **Eric Matthew Frein**, born 1983, **31**, lived with his parents in **Canadensis, Barrett Township**. ✅
- Member of a **military reenactment group that portrayed Eastern European (Cold War–era) soldiers**; police said he "appears to have assumed that role in real life." ✅ (CS Monitor called him a "self-styled Cold War soldier")
- Several trips to Southeast Europe. ✅ Hard drive showed **research on evading police manhunts**, law-enforcement technology and survival skills. ✅ (WESA; CBS "researched police manhunts")
- Father: **Eugene Michael Frein**; testified at penalty phase that he shared anti-government views with his son but never encouraged targeting police. ✅ ❌ Do NOT say "retired Army"/"Serbian" — unverified.
- Added to the **FBI's Ten Most Wanted** list during the manhunt ⚠️ (widely reported; confirm exact date before using "Ten Most Wanted").

## 5. The manhunt — 48 days ✅
- **More than 1,000** law-enforcement officers at times (estimates "nearly 1,000" to "more than 1,000"). ✅
- Early focus: about **5 square miles** of rugged terrain around his parents' home in Pike and Monroe counties; later narrowed to the **Snow Hill Falls** neighborhood bordering a state forest. ✅
- Schools closed at times, outdoor events canceled, roads blockaded, residents told to stay home; Halloween moved indoors. ✅ Barrett Township's police chief: the search was more than his **six-officer** department could imagine. ✅
- **Sept 29**: investigators found a **hastily abandoned campsite** — journal pages (damp, crumpled, in a trash bag), food, clothing, ammunition, explosives; his **checkbook and cellphone** suggested he left in a hurry. ✅ (date per affidavit)
- **Two fully functional pipe bombs** with **trip wires and fuses** found in the woods; Lt. Col. **George Bivens** said Frein likely abandoned them "under pressure." ✅
- **Sightings**: at least one credible sighting **75–100 yards away through thick woods**; police couldn't close in. ✅
- Journal entries: an Oct 10 entry reading "**30 days**"; another "**slept like a human first time in two weeks**"; tone of weariness and surprise at how long the manhunt lasted. ✅ (UPI, NBC)
- Cost: about **$11 million** to State Police alone ($6.9M overtime). ✅

## 6. The capture — Thursday, October 30, 2014, about 6 p.m. ✅
- **U.S. Marshals** task force on a sweep at the **closed Birchwood-Pocono Airpark** near Tannersville (Monroe County), about **30 miles** from the barracks. ✅ (some officers first went to the wrong airport — WNEP ⚠️)
- Task force commander **Scott Malkowski**: "**I saw the black hat**" — spotted a figure moving from the woods toward the abandoned hangar. "**We just had a hunch that if we were on the run, this is a place we would hide.**" ✅ Deputy Marshal **Scott Kimball** also on scene.
- Not the result of a tip or sighting (TNOnline) ⚠️
- Frein was **unarmed**; weapons were in the hangar (a rifle was later tested). Malkowski ordered him to the ground, asked his name — he said "**Eric Frein**." No fight, no attempt to flee. ✅
- Facial injuries: marshals said they happened while he was face down on the pavement during the arrest (police first said otherwise). ✅ — mention neutrally or skip.
- Conflicts: inside the hangar vs. open field yards from it ⚠️ → script says "near an abandoned hangar."
- Found at/near the hangar: a computer storage drive with the "**Mom and Dad**" letter — "**The time seems right for a spark to ignite a fire in the hearts of men**"; only a revolution could "get us back the liberties we once had." Created Dec 2013, last accessed Oct 2014. ✅
- That night he gave a **videotaped confession**: called it an assassination meant to "**wake people up**." ✅

## 7. After
- Convicted on **all 12 charges** (incl. first-degree murder of a law-enforcement officer, terrorism, weapons of mass destruction) after a two-week trial, April 2017. ✅
- **April 26, 2017**: jury sentenced him to **death**; found no mitigating circumstances. ✅ Dickson's widow testified about raising their two sons; said she is "angry" now. ✅ (name not confirmed — don't name her)
- **Pa. Supreme Court upheld** conviction and death sentence (2019); **U.S. Supreme Court declined** his appeal (Jan 2020). ✅
- Pennsylvania hasn't executed anyone since **1999** ✅; moratorium under Gov. Wolf ✅. Current governor's stance — ❌ verify before using.
- On death row at SCI Greene. ⚠️

## 8. Saylo angle
- **The question:** how did one man stay hidden for 48 days from 1,000+ officers — and how did it end?
- **Hidden thing:** he'd been rehearsing this for years (reenactor living the role, manhunt research, supply caches) — the search was chasing a man who planned to be chased.
- **Wrong answer/pressure:** sightings at 75–100 yards and still nothing; pipe bombs on the trails meant searchers couldn't move fast.
- **The break:** not a tip — a marshal's hunch about where *he'd* hide, and a black hat crossing a field.
- **Ordinary truth ending:** the elaborate "revolution" ended face down on pavement saying his own name — and he rode back to Blooming Grove in Dickson's car, in Dickson's cuffs.
- **Planted detail:** the barracks parking lot / Dickson's patrol car (he was walking to it when shot) → pays off when Frein is driven back in it.
