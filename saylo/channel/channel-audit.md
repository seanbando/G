## Findings
1. **One video = 75% of all views.** Subscribers flat at 63–65 since Oct 12.
2. **Topic drift killed momentum.** Started with manhunts, then WW1, heists, Escobar, MH370, Sully, Boston. YouTube couldn't find an audience for the follow-ups.
3. **Winning titles hid the subject** ("The Car That…", "Nobody Knew…"). Losers named over-covered subjects (MH370, Escobar, Gardner heist) and competed with huge channels. "SECOND 9/11" overpromised.
4. **Sniper script = the formula:** cold open on a strange concrete detail ("Call me God"), one question the video answers ("where was the shooter?"), planted payoffs ("hold on to that"), a reveal that reframes the case (the car *was* the weapon), an ordinary-truth ending.
5. **Boston script** is well written but structured as a timeline with an abstract thesis and a low-stakes first 15 seconds. Raw "um… uh…" news audio left in near 10:50.
6. **Shorts work** (~10x recent long-form views) and stopped.
