| | Uncovering — "The 5-Day Manhunt for Luigi Mangione" | fern — NAR / religion & power | Nexpo — Egon / AllatRa |

| Subscribe ask | none | none | none |

Saylo's sniper video (~21 min, ~3,200 words) matches Uncovering's density. **Uncovering is the closest competitor.**

**Hooks:** all three show something concrete within 10 seconds, state the promise within 90 seconds, and hand the viewer one strange specific detail (Monopoly money / ice castle / "his hands").
- Uncovering: in medias res ("At 6:45 a.m., a bullet strikes…"), host at the real location, then flashback ("The story begins 10 days before").
- fern: thesis line, then a cinematic absurd scene, then present-day stakes, then explicit promise ("This documentary exposes…").
- Nexpo: date-stamped nostalgia ("Wildfires… Barbenheimer"), skeptic framing, a single hook object.

**Structure:** Uncovering = strict timestamp spine + small open loops ("these granola bars became key evidence"). fern = escalating acts + expert voice every few minutes + jargon sidebars. Nexpo = rabbit-hole layers + silent visual reveal sequences.

**Language:** Uncovering plain (grade 6–8). fern dry and punchy with short cliff sentences ("But that man was dying."). Nexpo casual first person with self-talk ("Okay, okay… No. Hold on.").

**Transitions:** timestamps; contradiction pivots ("this is where the story has conflicting reports"); year jumps; cliffhanger last lines; "Now, where were we?" after ads.

**Endings:** Uncovering = irony ("It wasn't a tactical raid… It was a few customers eating breakfast") — same as Saylo's ordinary-truth ending.

**Steal:** Uncovering's timestamp spine and irony payoff; fern's second voice (real 911/bodycam/presser/expert every few minutes); Nexpo's "one thing kept me here" hook object in the first 3 minutes.
**Skip:** 50-minute runtimes.

## 3. Thumbnails (fern, Uncovering, third channel — Nov 2026)
- Gray/desaturated base + one saturated accent (usually red; blue as a counter-color for "vs" setups).

- 2–3 words, heavy condensed font, white + one accent word, sometimes in box labels.
- One white hand-drawn arrow or red circle on the key detail.
- The Pizza Hut thumbnail (ordinary building at night, in the woods) validated the Saylo dumpster concept.
