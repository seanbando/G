Michael Jordan Was Never Cut (myth bust)

Michael Jordan was never cut from his high school team, and the real story is even better.

Picture tryouts at Laney High. A skinny sophomore, around five foot ten, wants a varsity spot.

The coach has room for just one sophomore. He picks Leroy Smith, six foot seven, because the team badly needs height.

Jordan goes to junior varsity, like almost every sophomore at his school. Technically, nobody cut him.

He took it personally anyway.

On JV, he dropped multiple forty-point games. The next year, he made varsity.

A few years later, he hit the game-winning shot for North Carolina in the national championship. Then he became Michael Jordan.

Decades later, in his Hall of Fame speech, he still called it getting cut, and pointed out Leroy Smith sitting in the crowd.

The cut never happened. The chip on his shoulder did.
