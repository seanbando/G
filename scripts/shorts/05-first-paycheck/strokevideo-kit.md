## Exact words (paste into "Add the exact words"; each paragraph = one board)
Numbers are written with $ and % here so subtitles and drawings show digits. The words match the recording.

```
You just got hired at $20 an hour, you worked 80 hours, and your first paycheck should be $1,600.

Then you open it, and it says about $1,369.

Wait, who took $230 bucks from my check?

Picture your paycheck as a $1,600 pizza, and three people grab a slice before you get a single bite. Social Security takes 6.2%, $99.20.

Medicare grabs 1.45%, another $23.20. Then federal income tax takes about $108, and in most states, your state grabs a slice too.

So it feels like you just got robbed, but those first two slices are how you earn the right to collect Social Security and Medicare when you're older.

The tax slice is only an estimate, and if too much gets taken, you get the difference back as a refund.

And here's the part nobody tells you, because your boss pays another $122.40 on top, a matching slice you never even see.

You thought your pizza got smaller, but it was actually bigger than you ever saw.
```

The pizza lines are grouped into two paragraphs so the pizza stays on the same board while slices come off (the hub-board idea), instead of a new board every sentence.
