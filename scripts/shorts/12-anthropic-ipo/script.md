Why You Didn't Get Anthropic at $83

Anthropic's stock was priced at eighty-three dollars, and most people couldn't buy it at that price.

Before a stock starts trading, banks sell I P O shares at a set price, and big funds usually get most of them.

This time up to a fifth went to regular investors through apps like Robinhood, but orders ran about six times the shares, so most got a few or none.
