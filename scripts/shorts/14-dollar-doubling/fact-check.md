# Fact-check: You're One Dollar That Doubles Every Day (Nov 20, 2026)

Structure: POV ("you" are the dollar), a ladder of scenes, a "one more doubling" reveal, the catch, and a question.
Originality: the "dollar doubling vs world wealth" idea has been done by another creator. Our angle is different: the bills are stacked physically (kitchen → building → space → Moon), with a POV narrator, and the ending question is a debate.

Bill thickness: 0.0043 in (0.109 mm). Source: Bureau of Engraving and Printing currency FAQ (archived moneyfactory.com), repeated by Wikipedia and others.
Stack height = 2^day bills x 0.0043 in.
