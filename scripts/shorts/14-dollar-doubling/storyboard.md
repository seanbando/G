# Storyboard (POV): You're One Dollar That Doubles Every Day

Script: script.md (158 words, about 49 s at 196 wpm).
Times are estimates; after the voiceover is recorded, every beat gets locked to its measured word.

Structure: POV ("you" are the dollar). A climbing ladder of scenes, then the "one more doubling" reveal, then the catch, then a debate question. One structure only.

Rules applied:
- Frame 1 is the hook scene itself, not a text card. Hook ("Every day, you double") done by about 2 s.
- A new scene every 3-4 s; something moves on every key word; the camera is never still.
- Running image: THE STACK. The camera rides the top of the stack like an elevator, and every step up is a new world.
- A day counter (top-left, a flip-clock style tile) ticks on every "Day" line.
- Ending: a debate question with two buttons for comments, then a save card and a follow card.

## The main character: YOU (the dollar bill)
- A crisp green $1 bill with big cartoon eyes, eyebrows, a mouth and little stick arms and legs. Our own design, not a copy of the real bill art: the portrait oval holds YOU's face, and "1" is in the corners.
- YOU always rides on top of the stack, so we see YOU and the world below.
- Faces: smug, excited, nervous, screaming with joy, scared of heights, awe, mischievous, guilty, deflated, old, questioning.
- Copies: every doubling, YOU "pops" into two (cell-split animation with a pop sound), and the copies stack underneath.

## Look and sound
- Colours: scene-by-scene, getting darker and grander as the stack climbs. Warm kitchen → blue city dusk → black space with stars → silver moonlight → red alarm glow when the world breaks → calm green for the stock market part.
- Particles: dust, then clouds, then stars, then moon dust, then sparks and cracks.
- Moves: rising crane shots, tilt-downs to show the height, whips between scenes, shake on every "pop" and impact.
- Sound: a pop on every doubling, a rising whoosh as the stack climbs, wind at altitude, silence in space, a bass hit when the world breaks. Master at -13 LUFS.
