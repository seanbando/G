# Research: How Many Days Until $1 Owns the World

Part 1 of the script system. Verified material only; the script may use nothing beyond this file.

Research date: November 12, 2026.

## 1. The premise

- Start with one dollar on day 0. It doubles every day, so on day *n* it is worth 2ⁿ dollars.
- This is a thought experiment. No real investment doubles every day (see section 5).

## 3. Total world wealth

- **About $517 trillion of global personal wealth at the end of 2025**, across the 56 markets UBS covers.
  - Source: UBS Global Wealth Report 2026, published June 30, 2026, as summarized on UBS's own report page (found by web search). A second compilation (BusinessStats) describes it as "more than 500 trillion dollars."
  - The UBS site itself couldn't be opened from this session, so the exact figure was confirmed through search results quoting UBS, not the PDF. **[Re-check against the report PDF before publishing.]**
- **Growth:** global personal wealth rose 10.8% in USD terms in 2025, after 4.6% in 2024 and 4.2% in 2023 (UBS, June 30, 2026).
- **Scope caveat:** UBS measures household (personal) wealth in 56 markets. It is the standard "global wealth" figure, but it is not literally every asset on Earth. Script wording should be "global personal wealth" or "all the personal wealth UBS counts worldwide," not "everything that exists."

## 4. Where the doubling dollar crosses each milestone

- **Day 40 passes the richest person alive.** Forbes' November 2026 top-ten ranking (data as of November 1, 2026) puts Elon Musk at about **$869 billion**; the day-40 value is about $1.1 trillion, and the day-39 value (about $550 billion) is still below him.
  - **[VOLATILE]** Forbes reported his fortune fell about $68 billion in the month before. Re-check the day before recording; the day-40 claim holds as long as his net worth stays between about $550 billion and $1.1 trillion.
  - Musk has been the world's richest person since May 2024 (Forbes).
- **Day 40 is still a tiny share of the world:** $1.1 trillion ÷ $517 trillion ≈ **0.2%**, so "less than one percent."
- **Day 48 is just over half:** $281 trillion ÷ $517 trillion ≈ **54%**.
- **Day 49 passes all of it:** about $563 trillion, more than $517 trillion.
- **The answer is robust:** day 49 stays correct for any world-wealth figure between about $281 trillion and $563 trillion.

## 5. The reality check

- **S&P 500 long-run return:** about **10.6% a year with dividends reinvested, 1957 through 2025** (Aswath Damodaran, NYU Stern annual data, as cited in search results). DQYDJ's calculator gives about 10.4% from April 1957 to April 2025. After inflation the long-run figure is roughly 6.5%.
- **The same dollar at a steady 10% a year** would need **about 356 years** to pass $517 trillion (calculated). At 10.6%, about 337 years. Script wording: "around three hundred fifty years" at "roughly ten percent," labelled an illustration, not a promise.
- Returns vary a lot year to year; a steady rate is a simplification.

## 6. Unverified or excluded

- Any claim about what "most people guess." No source, so **excluded**.
- "Own the world" literally. Wealth on paper isn't ownership of every asset or country, so the title can use it, but the narration says "personal wealth."
