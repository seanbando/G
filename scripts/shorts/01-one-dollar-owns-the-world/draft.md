How Many Days Until $1 Owns the World

This is how many days it takes for one dollar to grow bigger than all the personal wealth in the world if it doubles every single day. Make a guess now, before the answer shows up on screen.

On day ten, the dollar is worth one thousand twenty-four dollars. On day twenty, it is worth just over one million dollars. On day thirty, it is worth just over one billion dollars.

On day forty, the dollar is worth about one point one trillion dollars. That is more than Elon Musk, the richest person in the world, who Forbes valued at about eight hundred sixty-nine billion dollars at the start of November twenty twenty-six. Even so, it is less than one percent of the world's personal wealth.

UBS estimated global personal wealth at about five hundred seventeen trillion dollars at the end of twenty twenty-five. On day forty-eight, the dollar reaches about two hundred eighty-one trillion dollars, which is just over half. On day forty-nine, it is worth more than all of it.

No real investment doubles every day. Over long periods, the S and P five hundred has historically returned roughly ten percent a year with dividends reinvested, before inflation. At a steady ten percent a year, the same dollar would need around three hundred fifty years.
