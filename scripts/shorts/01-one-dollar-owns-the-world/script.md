How Many Days Until $1 Owns the World

Ten days in, your dollar is worth one thousand twenty-four dollars. Day twenty pushes it past one million, and day thirty puts you over one billion dollars, which is where this stops feeling like normal money.

Day forty means one point one trillion dollars, enough to pass the richest person alive, Elon Musk, whom Forbes put at eight hundred sixty-nine billion in early November twenty twenty-six.

UBS put global personal wealth at five hundred seventeen trillion dollars for twenty twenty-five. Day forty-eight gets you about two hundred eighty-one trillion, just past half, and one more doubling on day forty-nine leaves your dollar worth more than all of it.

No real investment doubles every day, and at the stock market's long-run average of roughly ten percent a year before inflation, the same trip takes around three hundred fifty years.
