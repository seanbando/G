You Built a Black Market With AI (fiction + true story)

You ask an AI to help you build a store nobody can shut down.

Banks block anything shady, so you only take Bitcoin.

Buyers don't trust strangers, so the AI adds a system that holds the money until the package arrives.

Scammers creep in, so it adds seller ratings, just like a normal shopping site.

Orders explode. Millions move through your site, and you're sure nobody can trace you.

But every Bitcoin payment sits on a public record forever, and the plans you typed into that AI could still be sitting in a chat log.

That's when the knock comes.

This really happened, minus the AI. A man named Ross Ulbricht ran a site called Silk Road, with about two hundred million dollars in sales.

An old forum post helped lead investigators to him. He was arrested in a public library and sentenced to two life sentences.

"Anonymous" money always leaves a receipt. Congrats, you just rebuilt Silk Road.
