# Storyboard: Same $1,000, Same S&P 500, 3 Different Endings (times estimated at ~175 wpm, ~58 s)

## Characters (identical on every board)
- KID 1 "the seller": boy about 12, short spiky RED hair, RED hoodie with a big white "1" on the chest, jeans, white sneakers. Always holds or stands beside his SHOEBOX after he sells.
- KID 2 "the late one": girl about 12, curly BLACK hair in a high puff, YELLOW t-shirt with a big black "2", denim overalls, a wristwatch (she's always checking the time).
- KID 3 "the holder": boy about 12, neat brown hair, round glasses, GREEN t-shirt with a big white "3", khaki shorts. Calm, hands in pockets.
- DAD (board 1 only): tall, short beard, grey sweater.
- YOU (board 2 only): the channel reference sheet (messy black hair, black square glasses, black suit, white shirt, thin black tie).
Kids always stand in the same order left to right: 1, 2, 3. Each kid's color is used for their bar/line on charts.
