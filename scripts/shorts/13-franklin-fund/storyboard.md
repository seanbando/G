# Storyboard: Ben Franklin's 200-Year Money Chest

Rule for this video: every line is a new 3D set, with new models, new character expressions, real movement, and details that act out the words.
Long lines are split into 2-3 shots (about 1-1.5 s each), so a new shot lands every 1-3 s.
Times are estimates until the voiceover is measured; every action then gets locked to its word with speech-to-text.

Look: 2.5D. 3D sets, props, lights and particles, with flat 2D characters whose faces change on cue.
The characters and sets are our own designs, not copied from any channel.
Each era gets its own light colour:
- 1790: candle amber
- 1890: gaslight green-gold
- 1990: neon magenta and cyan

Camera is always moving: push-ins, orbits, whips, and handheld shake on impacts.
Running image: the iron chest, the same model in every era, getting older and more beat up.

Cast (new sprites, each with an expression set):
- FRANKLIN: our cartoon, with round glasses, grey hair and a brown coat. Faces: smug, writing, excited, frown, shocked, facepalm, whisper.
- CLERK (1790): Boston town official in a tricorn hat. Faces: proud, serious, bored.
- BLACKSMITH (1790): young tradesman in a leather apron. Faces: hopeful grin, happy.
- BORROWER (1800s): sneaky, holds a pouch.
- TREASURER (1890): mustache and top hat. Faces: proud, then let down.
- OFFICIAL (1990): suit and brick phone. Faces: anticipation, stunned.
- SAM (from three friends): today's viewer stand-in. Faces: tempted, unsure.

## Board 1 · 0:00-0:03.4 · Hook
VO: "Ben Franklin gave Boston money to grow for two hundred years. Guess what it became."

- Frame 1 is a finished slam card: "$4,500 → 200 YEARS → ?" over the blurred chest.
- Set: dark 1790 study. Oak desk, quill and inkpot, a flickering candle (warm point light), a map of Boston pinned to the wall, dust in the candle light.
- On "Ben Franklin": FRANKLIN (smug half-lids) signs a parchment will. Quill scratch, and the wax seal stamps down.
- On "money": he drops a coin purse into the iron chest. Coins spill and glint.
- On "two hundred years": the lid slams, the padlock snaps shut, and a carved "200 YEARS" brands into the lid with sparks. Shake.
- On "Guess": whip-pan to a big neon "?" and Franklin winks.
- Camera: wide, then a fast push-in to the chest, then a whip.

## Board 2 · ~0:03.4-0:07 · 1790
VO: "Seventeen ninety. A thousand pounds, about forty-five hundred dollars, goes into the chest."

- Set: 1790 Boston harbor street at dusk. Cobblestones, tall-ship masts, lanterns on posts, fog rolling.
- On "1790": a wooden sign reading "1790" swings down on chains.
- On "a thousand pounds": a 3D stack of coins labelled "£1,000" rises out of the chest, then the label flips to "≈ $4,500".
- CLERK (proud) rides a horse-drawn cart carrying the chest toward the town hall.
- Camera: crane down from the masts, then dolly alongside the cart.

## Board 3 · ~0:07-0:11 · The rule (2 shots)
VO: "The rule: lend it to young tradesmen at five percent, and keep lending the interest."

1. Blacksmith forge. Glowing anvil, sparks, orange rim light. CLERK (serious) hands the BLACKSMITH (hopeful grin) a coin pouch, and a tag swings on it: "LOAN · 5%".
2. On "keep lending the interest": pull back to a mini Boston diorama. Glowing coins travel from the blacksmith back to the chest, then out to a carpenter, then a printer, then back again: a loop of light circling the town. Coins get added to the loop on each pass.

- Camera: orbit the anvil, then a fast pull-back to the diorama.

## Board 4 · ~0:11-0:16 · Franklin's math (2 shots)
VO: "Franklin did the math himself. One hundred years, a hundred thirty-one thousand pounds. Two hundred years, over four million."

1. Study. FRANKLIN (excited, glasses glinting) scribbles on a parchment, and ink numbers float up off the page.
2. On "one hundred years": a gold tower rises from the floor, labelled "100 YRS: £131,000". On "two hundred years": a second tower shoots up through the ceiling and the roof, labelled "200 YRS: £4,061,000". Franklin points up, grinning.

- Camera: tilt up following the tower out through the roof, with roof debris and a shake.

## Board 5 · ~0:16-0:20 · 1890, first opening
VO: "Eighteen ninety. The chest opens. Three hundred ninety-one thousand dollars. Only two thirds of his math."

- Set: 1890 city hall vault. Gas lamps (green-gold light), brass fittings, polished floor, a portrait of Franklin on the wall.
- On "1890": a calendar flips 1790 → 1890 in a blur of pages.
- On "the chest opens": the lid creaks up, a light beam shoots out, and dust swirls in it. TREASURER (proud) leans in.
- On "$391,000": a real money pile with that label. Beside it stands a translucent "ghost" pile, Franklin's math (£131,000 ≈ $582,000). The real pile fills it to a 67% line.
- On "two thirds": TREASURER's face drops. The Franklin portrait on the wall changes from smile to frown.
- Camera: push in on the chest, then a whip to the portrait.

## Board 6 · ~0:20-0:23 · Trade school
VO: "Part of it builds a trade school. The rest goes back in the chest."

- Set: an early-1900s Boston street under construction. Scaffolding, brick piles, a crane, workers.
- On "builds a trade school": bricks stack themselves in a timelapse, the building rises, and a sign bolts on: "FRANKLIN UNION · 1908".
- On "the rest": a smaller coin stream pours back into the chest, and the lid shuts.
- Camera: timelapse crane up with the building, then a cut to the chest.

## Board 7 · ~0:23-0:25.5 · 1990, second opening
VO: "Nineteen ninety. The chest opens again."

- Set: 1990 Boston at night. Neon signs, skyscrapers, a passing car's headlights; inside, a modern vault with a CRT monitor reading "FRANKLIN FUND". The chest, now scuffed and ancient, sits on a pedestal under a spotlight.
- On "1990": a digital clock flips, from 1890 straight to 1990.
- OFFICIAL (anticipation, holding a brick phone) reaches for the lid. A drum-roll build, and the lid lifts slowly.
- Camera: slow push-in with handheld tension.

## Board 8 · ~0:25.5-0:29.5 · The reveal
VO: "Almost five million dollars. Less than a third of what his math promised."

- On "almost five million dollars": a burst of light, and the real pile "≈ $5,000,000" rises. OFFICIAL (stunned) grins.
- On "less than a third": pull back to reveal a giant translucent ghost tower behind it, Franklin's math, "£4,061,000 promised". The real pile only reaches a quarter of the way up, and a "~25%" meter marks it.
- The ghost of FRANKLIN rises above the chest, shocked, his glasses sliding down his nose.
- Camera: a scale-reveal pull-back, then a small shake.

## Board 9 · ~0:29.5-0:35 · Why (3 shots, one per reason)
VO: "Fewer tradesmen borrowed, many didn't pay back, and the money got parked in safe, slow accounts."

1. "Fewer tradesmen borrowed": the loan desk sits empty and the CLERK (bored) taps his fingers. The wall clock spins fast and cobwebs grow.
2. "Many didn't pay back": at night, a BORROWER (sneaky) tiptoes away with a pouch. A red "UNPAID" stamp slams onto an IOU.
3. "Safe, slow accounts": coins slide into a vault marked "SAVINGS". A snail crawls across a growth meter that barely moves. The money pile yawns, as a cartoon detail.

- Camera: a fast push-in on each shot, with whip transitions between them.

## Board 10 · ~0:35-0:38 · The lesson
VO: "The math never failed. People interrupted it."

- Set: abstract dark stage with a glowing gold compounding curve climbing smoothly in 3D, and particles flowing along it.
- On "never failed": the curve keeps climbing and FRANKLIN nods.
- On "people interrupted it": hands come in from the sides with giant scissors and snip the curve. It breaks and falls. Impact shake. FRANKLIN facepalms.
- Camera: dolly along the curve, then stop dead on the cut.

## Board 11 · ~0:38-0:45 · Ending question
VO: "So could you leave your money alone for twenty years, let alone two hundred?"

- Set: today, a small apartment bedroom. Fairy lights, a desk, a phone showing a stock app balance, and a tiny version of the chest on the shelf.
- SAM (tempted) hovers his thumb over a big red "SELL" button on the phone. FRANKLIN's ghost appears behind his shoulder (whispering) and shakes his head.
- On "twenty years": "20 YEARS?" pops up. On "two hundred": "200?" stamps over it, and a big "?" lands.
- Camera: push in to SAM's face, then a drifting hold.

- The voiceover is measured with speech-to-text and every action is cued to its word.
- Camera never still (under 5% still frames), brightness about 90-110, master at -13 LUFS, captions burned in last.
