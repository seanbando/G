# Storyboard (POV): You Are the Money

Script: script-pov.md (146 words, about 45 s at 196 wpm).
Times are estimates until the voiceover is recorded and measured with speech-to-text; every beat is then locked to its word.

Structure: POV (second person, "you" are the thousand pounds), told in order through 1791 → 1891 → 1991. One structure only.

Rules applied:
- Frame 1 is the hook scene itself, not a text card. The hook lands by about 3 s, and the visuals act out every key word.
- New set or new action every 0.6-1.5 s; camera never still (under 5% still frames).
- Ending: Yes / No buttons (comments), then a save card and a follow card.

## The main character: YOU (the coin)
- A chunky gold coin with big cartoon eyes, eyebrows and a mouth. It stands for the whole thousand pounds.
- **Its size shows its value, all the way through:**

| Point | Value | Size |
|---|---|---|
| 1791 | about $4,500 | fits in a palm |
| 1891 | $391,000 | as tall as a person |
| 1991 | about $5,000,000 | fills the vault |

- Next to it, always: a translucent "ghost coin" for Franklin's math, getting bigger faster. The gap between the two coins is the whole story.
- Faces: wide-eyed, nervous, determined, happy, bored, sigh, squint, proud, shocked, deflated, smug, questioning.
- POV shots: several shots are from the coin's eyes (lens rim, slight fisheye), for example looking up at the lid slamming, or out of a pouch at the blacksmith.

## Other cast (2D sprites, faces swap on cue)
- FRANKLIN: brown coat, round glasses. Faces: smug, scribbling, proud, worried ghost.
- CLERK (1791): tricorn hat.
- BLACKSMITH (1791): leather apron. Faces: hopeful, happy.
- STRUGGLING BORROWER (1800s): sweating, empty pockets.
- TREASURER (1891): top hat and mustache.
- WORKERS (1900s).
- OFFICIAL (1991): suit and brick phone.
- SAM (today): from three friends.

## Look and sound
- Era light colours:

| Era | Light | Colours |
|---|---|---|
| 1791 | candle amber | #ffb35c |
| 1891 | gaslight green-gold | #d8c060 |
| 1991 | neon magenta / cyan | #ff3fa4 / #3fe0ff |
| inside the chest | dark blue, with light leaking through the cracks | |

- Particles: dust, sparks, coin glints.
- Moves: push-ins, whips between eras, shake on every hit.
- Sound: a soft "voice" for the coin (tiny boings and squeaks on its face changes), coin clinks on every growth, room tone for each era. Master at -13 LUFS.
