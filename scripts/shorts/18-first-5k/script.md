Jay, Maya and Sam: The Same $5,000

One friend never lost a dollar, and she still got poorer.

Jay, Maya and Sam each get five thousand dollars.

"Life's short," Jay says, and books a week in Miami.

Maya puts hers in regular savings. "It can't go down," she says.

Sam puts his in an S and P five hundred fund and forgets about it.

Then prices start climbing. Groceries, rent, gas.

Then a bad year hits. Sam's fund drops about eighteen percent. Maya laughs. Hers never dropped a cent.

Jay has Miami photos. That's it.

By the end of twenty twenty-five, Maya has just over five thousand. Sam has almost ninety-eight hundred.

But to buy what five thousand bought before, you now need sixty-two hundred.

So Maya didn't lose money. She lost what her money can buy.

Savings is for emergencies. Long-term money has to outgrow prices.

Which friend are you right now, Jay, Maya or Sam?
