Why the Friend Who Quit Ended Up Richest

Four friends saved the same hundred dollars a month, and forty years later, one had forty-eight thousand while another had over four hundred thousand.

YOU: Wait, how did the same hundred bucks do that?

The first hid it under his mattress, and at sixty-two, he had forty-eight thousand, not a dollar more.

The second used a savings account paying four percent, and ended with about one hundred eighteen thousand.

The third waited until thirty-two, then invested in an index fund averaging about ten percent a year, for thirty years, and ended with about two hundred twenty-six thousand.

The fourth started that same index fund at twenty-two, and after ten years, he quit.

His money had ten extra years, and his gains kept earning their own gains, which is called compounding.
