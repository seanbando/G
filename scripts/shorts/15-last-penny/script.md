You're America's Last Penny (POV)

You're America's last penny. You cost almost four cents to make.

November twelfth, twenty twenty-five. A press in Philadelphia slams down, and two hundred thirty-two years of everyday pennies end with you.

Every penny cost the Mint three point six nine cents. In twenty twenty-four, that lost about eighty-five million dollars.

But the real problem? You got weak.

Nineteen thirteen: a penny like you buys what over thirty cents buys today.

Nineteen fifty: about fourteen cents.

Nineteen eighty: about four.

Today: almost nothing. Stores round you away, and people step over you on the sidewalk.

Same coin. Same "one cent" on your face. Prices kept climbing, so decade after decade you bought less.

That's inflation. And your dollar is on the same road. Since nineteen thirteen, it's lost about ninety-seven percent of its buying power.

So, would you bend down to pick up a penny? Yes or no.
