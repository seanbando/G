The Red Candle (mirror format: but... so..., ordinary person ending)

The market opens, and your stock drops so fast it prints a giant red candle, so you think discount and buy more.

But it keeps falling, down twenty percent by lunch, so you buy even more to lower your average.

But it drops again, and your stomach drops with it, so you sell everything just to make the pain stop.

Then it rips straight back up, and everyone online is posting their gains, so you panic and buy back in at the top.

You bought high, sold low, and bought high again, all before dinner.

Then your grandpa calls. He bought the same stock years ago, never once opened the app, and he's still up.
