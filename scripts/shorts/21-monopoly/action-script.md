# Action Script: You're a Monopoly Board (POV)

Each voice line has its action underneath: what moves, faces, camera and sound. A new action lands every 1-2 s; the camera is never still. Times are estimates until the voiceover is measured.

Characters:
- THE BOARD (the "you"): a Monopoly-style board with big cartoon eyes and a mouth in the center, little arms out of two sides. Our own design: no real Monopoly logo, art or trademarked characters, just a square board with colored property strips.
- LIZZIE MAGIE: early-1900s woman, hair up, long dress, kind but serious.
- CHARLES DARROW: 1930s man, slicked hair, sweater vest, sly grin.
- PARKER BROTHERS EXECS: two men in suits and fedoras.

---

**VO:** "You're a Monopoly board, and you were stolen from the woman who invented you."
- **Frame 1 (finished shot):** THE BOARD sits on a kitchen table under a lamp, eyes wide, looking straight at the camera.
- **"you were stolen":** a hand snatches THE BOARD off the table. The camera whips to follow it, and THE BOARD looks back in shock as it's carried away.
- **"the woman who invented you":** freeze. A sepia flashback ripple reveals Lizzie's silhouette at a drawing desk.
- **Sound:** a quick grab whoosh, then a record scratch on the freeze.

**VO:** "Lizzie Magie built you as a warning about landlords, so every rent you charge and every player you bankrupt was meant to feel unfair."
- **"built you":** a 1900s study with lamplight. Lizzie (focused) paints THE BOARD's squares, and the board blinks awake as each strip of color appears.
- **"a warning about landlords":** a little landlord token in a top hat hops onto a square, and a "WARNING" stamp slams on.
- **"every rent you charge":** a tiny tenant token drops coins into the landlord's pile, and THE BOARD winces.
- **"every player you bankrupt":** a tenant token tips over, its pockets turned out, and "BANKRUPT" stamps red.
- **"meant to feel unfair":** Lizzie looks at the camera and nods. That's the point.
- **Camera:** push-in on the board, then a quick tilt to Lizzie's face.

**VO:** "Tucked inside your box was a second set of rules, one where everyone at the table gets richer together."
- **"Tucked inside your box":** the game box lid slides open. A glowing second rulebook rises out with a soft chime.
- **"everyone at the table gets richer together":** four player tokens around the board all grow small coin stacks at the same time, and everyone cheers. THE BOARD smiles.
- **Camera:** slow orbit around the table.

**VO:** "For years, people passed around homemade copies of you."
- **Action:** a fast montage of hands passing hand-drawn copies of THE BOARD, from house to house and kitchen to kitchen, as calendar pages fly. Each copy has slightly wobbly handwritten squares. THE BOARD (amused) watches its copies multiply.
- **Camera:** whip pans between houses.

**VO:** "That's when Charles Darrow played one and sold you to Parker Brothers as his own idea."
- **"Charles Darrow played one":** a 1930s living room. Darrow rolls the dice, then stops and rubs his chin with a sly grin. THE BOARD gets nervous.
- **"sold you to Parker Brothers":** a boardroom, where Darrow slaps THE BOARD on a big desk in front of the two execs.
- **"as his own idea":** Darrow taps his own head proudly, and a "MY IDEA" speech bubble pops up. THE BOARD's jaw drops.
- **Camera:** push-in on Darrow's grin, then a whip to the boardroom.
- **Sound:** dice roll, desk slap.

**VO:** "Darrow walked away a millionaire, while Lizzie handed over her patent for $500."
- **Split screen.** On the left, money rains on Darrow, a "$1,000,000" counter spins up, and he's grinning. On the right, Lizzie slides a single paper labelled "PATENT" across a desk and receives a small "$500" envelope.
- **On screen:** "1935"
- **Camera:** the split screen holds while the left side keeps getting busier. The contrast is the gut punch.
- **Sound:** a cash-register jackpot on the left, one sad coin drop on the right.

**VO:** "What came next was a game with its second rulebook torn out, teaching one lesson only, which is to crush everyone."
- **"second rulebook torn out":** an exec grabs the glowing rulebook and rips it in half, and the glow dies. THE BOARD looks sad.
- **"crush everyone":** a giant hand slams a hotel token onto a square, and three tenant tokens get flattened into coins. THE BOARD's eyes turn hard.
- **Camera:** hard punch-in with an impact shake.
- **Sound:** paper rip, then a heavy slam.

**VO:** "Built to warn people about monopolies, you became one."
- **"Built to warn people":** a quick flashback flicker of Lizzie painting the board.
- **"you became one":** a fast pull-back. THE BOARD's box is stacked on endless store shelves, then a world map fills with boxes, and THE BOARD stares at itself, stunned.
- **Camera:** a big pull-back for the scale reveal.
- **Sound:** a low boom.

**VO:** "Off the board, the people playing you live by that same lesson, because owners collect the rent and renters pay it."
- **"Off the board":** the camera lifts off the table into a real apartment hallway.
- **"owners collect the rent":** a landlord character opens an envelope full of cash.
- **"renters pay it":** a young renter (or SAM, if this goes in the Jay/Maya/Sam universe) slides a rent check under a door with a sigh. A small "RENT DUE" sticky note flaps.
- **Camera:** dolly down the hallway, past door after door.

**VO:** "Which side of that are you on in real life?"
- **Split door frame:** "COLLECTING RENT" on the left in green, "PAYING RENT" on the right in red, and a comment bubble wiggles.
- **Final beat:** THE BOARD peeks in from the corner and winks. Then hard cut back to frame 1 for a seamless loop.
- **Sound:** a double hit, then the loop.

---

On-screen text, small: "1904 patent" (during Lizzie's scene), "1935" (during the $500 split).
Rule: no real Monopoly logo, box art, Mr. Monopoly or other trademarked artwork. Our board is a generic look-alike with our own design.
