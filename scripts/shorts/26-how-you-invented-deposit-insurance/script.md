How You Invented Deposit Insurance ("How You Invented" series, Jay = troublemaker)

Everybody in town is pulling out at the same time.

It started when you opened a bank, and the whole town kept their money in your vault.

But the money just sat there, so you lent most of it out and earned interest. Clever.

Except Jay starts a rumor that your bank is broke, so everyone rushes in at once to pull their money out.

But most of it is lent out, so your vault runs dry and your bank goes under.

So the panic spreads, and banks all over the country collapse the same way.

"JUST LEAVE YOUR MONEY IN THE BANK!"

So the government makes a promise: if your bank fails, your deposits are covered, up to two hundred fifty thousand dollars. Genius.

Now most people have no reason to rush.

So most people don't panic when they pull out, unless they had way more than that in the bank. Just ask Silicon Valley Bank.
