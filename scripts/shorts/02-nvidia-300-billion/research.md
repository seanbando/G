# Research: One Man's Secret Just Cost Nvidia $300 Billion

Checked November 12, 2026. The story is still moving: recheck the board's decision right before posting.

- **Investigation:** Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation on November 8, 2026, after reports that Jensen Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort centred on a Chinese businesswoman (Alice Huang, no relation). No criminal charges reported. (NPR, Al Jazeera, Washington Post)
- **Admission:** Huang told staff in an internal message he was "unfaithful." (ABC News)
- **Second relationship:** the New York Times reported a separate relationship with an executive who reports to him. (via ABC, Fortune)
- **Board:** the Wall Street Journal reported the board is preparing to oust him; the board was meeting Wednesday, November 11. No decision announced as of November 12. The board hired Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz. (CNBC, CNN, Fortune)
- **Stock:** fell more than 9% after hours on the WSJ report (CNBC); other outlets reported 4 to 8% at different times. Over $300 billion in market value lost that week. (CNN)
- **Congress:** the House China committee asked Huang to a closed-door briefing on November 18. (Washington Post)
- **Succession:** Huang has said he does not believe in succession planning; CFO Colette Kress is named as a likely interim CEO. (Forbes / Newsweek coverage)
- **Tenure:** co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and has been CEO since, so over thirty years.
- **Apple:** Steve Jobs was pushed out of Apple in 1985; by 1997 Apple was close to bankruptcy.
- **Index funds:** Nvidia is one of the largest holdings in the S&P 500.
