# Research: How Elon Lost $80 Billion Without Spending a Dollar

Checked November 13, 2026.

- **One-day drop:** Musk's net worth fell $79.7 billion on Thursday, November 5, 2026 (Bloomberg Billionaires Index); Forbes put it at $79.3 billion. (Fortune, 24/7 Wall St., Forbes)
- **Two-day drop:** about $113 billion over two sessions per Bloomberg: $33.5 billion on Wednesday plus $79.7 billion on Thursday. (tech-insider summary of Bloomberg figures)
- **Share moves that day:** Tesla fell about 11% (10.77% close per Bloomberg-based reports, 11.5% per Forbes); SpaceX fell 8% to around $125 a share.
- **Why:** coverage ties it to investor worry about investigations and tighter regulation after the midterm elections, plus a wider tech selloff. (Fortune, CNN, NPR)
- **He did not sell shares:** the losses are paper estimates from share prices. (search summary of coverage)
- **Still the richest:** after the drop he stayed at the top of the rich list, around $785 to $790 billion. (Bloomberg, Forbes)
- **Paper wealth:** value of assets you own at today's price; it only becomes cash, or a realized loss, when you sell.
- **Not confirmed:** whether the shares have rebounded since. The script avoids saying he is still down.
