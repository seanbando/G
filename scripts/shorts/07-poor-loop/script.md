Why Being Broke Costs More

Being broke costs more than being rich, and it works like a rocket that can't escape Earth.

YOU: Wait, how does having less cost me more?

Then your car breaks down, so you grab a four hundred dollar payday loan, and two weeks later you owe four hundred sixty.

And that's not rare, because almost four in ten adults couldn't cover a four hundred dollar surprise with cash or savings.

Can't pay it back? You pay sixty more just to push it two weeks, and the weight stays chained to your rocket.

Miss one payment, your credit drops, and in most states even your car insurance costs more, so every launch gets heavier.
