Why Tesla Can't Fire Elon Musk

Tesla's board can legally fire Elon Musk tomorrow. They never will.

He's the biggest shareholder, and shareholders vote for the board.

Look who's on the board. One of the directors is his brother.

Then there's the pay deal. Last year, shareholders approved a package that could be worth around a trillion dollars, if Tesla hits huge targets.

Hit them all, and his stake could grow to about a quarter of the company.

But the real reason?

Much of Tesla's price is a bet on Musk himself: robotaxis, robots, AI. Fire him, and the stock could drop hard.

That's called key-person risk. One person becomes the stock.

So when you buy a stock, you're also buying the people running it.
