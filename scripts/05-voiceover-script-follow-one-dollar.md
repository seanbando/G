# Voice-Over Script: Follow $1 From Your Pocket to Wall Street: The Stock Market Explained

Voice-only recording sheet. Every spoken line is numbered (VO-01, VO-02 …) and becomes one audio file with the same number, so the files join back together in order. Golpo animates the finished narration, so there are no drawing notes here; those live in the main script and the Golpo brief.

**All numbers, dollar amounts, dates, times and abbreviations are written out in words**, so read them exactly as written.

## Cast: 9 voices

**Total:** 94 lines (one scene each) · 1905 spoken words · about 10.6 minutes of audio at the narrator's pace of 180 words per minute, before pauses. The longest scene is about 12 seconds.

**Pause-and-guess breaks:** the ⏸ cues are silent beats so viewers can guess. Nobody records them; the silence is added automatically when the files are joined.

### Recording tips
- **Every character gets its own voice,** so viewers can tell who is talking without seeing the drawing. **YOU** especially must never sound like the narrator.
- **Name each file after its line ID,** for example `VO-02_YOU.wav`, so the editor can line the audio up in order.
- **Don't worry about silence at the start or end of a file.** It's trimmed automatically when the files are joined.
- **Read every line as a full sentence.** Don't clip character lines into short fragments.
- **Letter-by-letter names:** "D-T-C-C" and "S-I-P-C" are written with hyphens so they're read one letter at a time, and "U.S." is read "you-ess".
