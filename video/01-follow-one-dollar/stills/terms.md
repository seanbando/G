# Plain-words cards on screen

Every term a viewer might not know gets a yellow "IN PLAIN WORDS" card on the still where it is first said.

| Still | Term | Stands for | Plain meaning on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| S014 | SHARE |  | One small piece of ownership in a company. |
| S015 | STOCK MARKET |  | The place where people buy and sell shares. |
| S028 | MARKET CAP |  | What the whole company is worth. Short for market capitalization. |
| S034 | FRACTIONAL SHARE |  | A piece of one share, for when you buy less than a whole one. |
| S035 | MARKET ORDER |  | An order to buy (or sell) right now, at whatever price is available. |
| S037 | BROKER |  | The company behind your investing app. It places your orders for you. |
| S041 | RETAIL ORDER |  | An order from an everyday investor like you, not from a big firm. |
| S042 | MARKET MAKER |  | A trading firm that is always ready to buy shares from you or sell shares to you. |
| S046 | SPREAD |  | The small gap between the buy price and the sell price. The market maker tries to earn it. |
| S049 | PAYMENT FOR ORDER FLOW |  | Market makers paying your broker to send your orders to them. |
| S050 | COMMISSION |  | A fee a broker charges you each time you trade. |
| S051 | BEST EXECUTION |  | Your broker's duty to try to get you the best deal that is reasonably available. |
| S053 | INVENTORY |  | Shares the market maker already owns and keeps ready to sell. |
| S055 | STOCK EXCHANGE |  | An organized marketplace where buyers and sellers of shares are matched. Most people picture it when they hear "Wall Street". |
| S056 | NYSE | New York Stock Exchange | The biggest U.S. stock exchange. Its building is on Wall Street in New York City. |
| S057 | DATA CENTER |  | A building full of computers. This is where the trades are really matched. |
| S058 | NASDAQ |  | The other big U.S. stock exchange. Many tech companies, including Apple, are listed on it. |
| S065 | EXTENDED HOURS |  | Trading before 9:30 a.m. or after 4 p.m., when far fewer people are trading. |
| S077 | IPO | Initial Public Offering | The first time a company sells its shares to the public. |
| S080 | FUND |  | A big pool of many people's money, invested together by a manager. |
| S085 | EXECUTIVES |  | The top bosses who run a company. |
| S088 | SETTLE |  | The trade is finished: the buyer has the shares and the seller has the money. |
| S088 | T+1 |  | Trade day plus 1 business day. That is when the trade settles. |
| S090 | DTCC | Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation | The behind-the-scenes group of companies that clears and settles stock trades in the U.S. |
| S091 | CLEAR |  | Check the trade and work out who owes what. |
| S091 | SETTLE |  | Swap the money and the shares. The trade is done. |
| S094 | CEDE & CO. |  | The name written on the official record for many shares that are held through brokerages. "Cede" is said like "seed". |
| S095 | DTC | Depository Trust Company | The DTCC company that holds the shares. On the official record it uses the name Cede & Co. |
| S098 | BENEFICIAL OWNER |  | The real owner. You get the gains and the losses, even though another name is on the official record. |
| S101 | SHAREHOLDER VOTE |  | People who own shares can vote on some big company decisions. |
| S114 | DIVIDEND |  | A payment a company makes to the people who own its shares, out of its profits. |
| S117 | LAST-TRADED PRICE |  | The price of the most recent sale: what a buyer just paid a seller. |
| S122 | EARNINGS |  | A company's profit: the money left after it pays its costs. |
| S128 | S&P 500 |  | A list of about 500 of the biggest U.S. companies. |
| S128 | INDEX FUND |  | A fund that simply buys everything on a list. |
| S130 | WEIGHT |  | How much of the basket each company takes up. Bigger company, bigger share of the basket. |
| S133 | RETURN |  | How much an investment gained or lost over a period, shown as a percent. |
| S134 | REINVESTED |  | Dividends used to buy more shares instead of being spent. |
| S134 | INFLATION |  | Everyday prices rising over time, so a dollar buys less. |
| S144 | MARKET CAP |  | Price × number of shares. What the whole company is worth. |
| S146 | ORDER ROUTING |  | Where your broker sends your order to get it filled. |
| S147 | SIPC | Securities Investor Protection Corporation | It protects customers if a brokerage firm fails. It does not protect against a stock falling. |
| S148 | CUSTOMER ASSETS |  | The shares and cash a brokerage holds for you. |
| S157 | NVIDIA |  | A company that designs computer chips, including chips used for AI. |
| S159 | CANDLESTICK CHART |  | A price chart made of bars. Each bar shows where the price started, ended, and its high and low. |
| S160 | FINANCIALLY FREE |  | Having enough money that you don't have to work just to pay the bills. |
| S162 | CANDLESTICK CHART |  | A price chart made of bars. Each bar shows where the price started, ended, and its high and low. |
